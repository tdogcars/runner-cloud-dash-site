FEATURES

Every screen in Runner POS was designed for busy hands and fast shifts.

FRONT OF HOUSE

Your floor, your way.

Build a floor plan that matches your dining room. Drag in tables of any shape, add landmarks like the bar, host stand, and patio, and set seats at every table. Servers see what's open, what's seated, and who owns each check at a glance.

  • Custom floor plan editor with table shapes, landmarks, and seats
  • Order by seat and keep every table's check organized
  • Switch to quick service for counter orders in a tap
  • Open, name, and manage bar tabs all night

ORDERING

Ring it in exactly how they ordered it.

Sizes, modifiers, and removable ingredients are built right into every item. Tap NO, EXTRA, or SIDE on any ingredient and the ticket reads clearly in the kitchen. Add item photos to POS buttons so new staff find things fast.

  • Sizes and modifier groups on any item
  • NO / EXTRA / SIDE on removable ingredients
  • Item photos on POS buttons
  • Clear, readable tickets for the kitchen and bar

KITCHEN DISPLAY

Tickets go where they belong.

Kitchen items go to the kitchen. Drinks go to the bar. Runner routes every order to the right screen the moment it's sent, and cooks bump tickets when they're done, so the line stays clean and nothing gets lost.

  • Kitchen and Bar routing, automatically
  • Bump tickets when they're up
  • Modifiers and ingredient changes shown clearly on every ticket
  • Works alongside kitchen printers if you still want paper

HANDHELDS

Take the POS to the table.

Servers take orders tableside on Runner handhelds. Orders reach the kitchen in seconds, and if the internet goes down, handhelds keep taking orders and save them until they sync.

  • Talk to your store hub station over your local network
  • Keep taking orders offline
  • Same menu, modifiers, and floor plan as the station
  • Fewer trips to the terminal, faster table turns

CHECKS & CLOSE-OUT

Split it, tip it, close it out.

Split checks by seat or item in a few taps. Collect tips, adjust tips after the fact, and batch close at the end of the night. Set up an automatic service fee once, and Runner applies it every time.

  • Split checks by seat or by item
  • Tips and tip adjust
  • Batch close at end of day
  • Automatic service fees

QUICK EDIT

86 it in two taps. Right from the station.

Out of the special? Price change on happy hour? Managers can update prices, 86 items, and adjust the button layout directly on the station with Quick Edit — no laptop, no waiting.

  • Change prices on the spot
  • 86 items instantly
  • Rearrange the POS layout during service

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Build your system and a local specialist will walk you through it.

What's the difference between Runner POS and Runner Cloud?

Runner POS is the app your team uses on stations and handhelds to take orders, run tables and tabs, and close checks. Runner Cloud is your back office in the browser, where you build menus, manage employees and permissions, set taxes, and run reports.

Does Runner work if my internet goes down?

Yes. Every station and handheld keeps taking orders and saves each change on the device. With a station set up as your store hub, your devices and kitchen screens keep talking over your restaurant's local network, so tickets still reach the kitchen. When the internet is back, everything syncs to Runner Cloud.

Is Runner for full service, quick service, or bars?

All three. Use table service with your own floor plan, ring up quick service orders at the counter, and keep bar tabs open — in the same system.

What hardware do I need?

Most restaurants start with at least one station and one receipt or kitchen printer. Add handhelds for tableside ordering and kitchen display screens for the line. Use the system builder to pick what fits and we'll put together a quote.

How much does it cost?

Every restaurant is different, so pricing is provided with your quote. Build your system and a local specialist will send you pricing for your exact setup.

Who installs and trains my staff?

A local Runner specialist installs your hardware, helps build your menu and floor plan, and trains your team on-site.

Can I manage more than one location?

Yes. Runner Cloud supports multiple locations with a shared menu and store-level overrides for items, prices, or availability that differ by location.

Can managers make menu changes during service?

Yes. With Quick Edit, managers can change prices, 86 items, and adjust the button layout right on the station. Bigger menu changes can be drafted in Runner Cloud and published when you're ready.

Can I set up automatic service fees?

Yes. Configure an automatic service fee and Runner applies it to checks for you.

What reports can I see?

Runner Cloud includes sales, product mix, sales by employee, sales by hour, and payments reports.
